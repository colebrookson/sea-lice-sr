## Components of the analysis: 

There are a number of items that need to be kept track of as I move things over from the previous versions: 


### Sources of Data

1. **Farm data** These data come from a variety of sources (BATI vs. Marty (2010) etc) and we use these data to get estimates of the number of lice on farmed fish / the numbers of farmed fish in the region of interest during the study period. 
2. **Fish data** These data come from SCFS and are samples of wild fish wherein the number of lice on wild fish is measured through time. 
3. **Spawner recruit data** The pink salmon spawner recruit data include data on the number of spawners which comes from NuSEDS, and then the estimates of recruitment that are made via estimating escapement. 

### Components of Analysis 

1. Relationship between lice on wild juvenile fish and fish on farmed salmon
2. The estimation of recruitment 
3. The population models
