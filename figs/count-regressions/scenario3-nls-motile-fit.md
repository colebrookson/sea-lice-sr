## Notes

Fig. S3 (Scenario 3). Asymptotic nls fit Y = 1 - exp(-c*X), a = 1 and b = 0 fixed, c estimated, relating the yearly pooled proportion of speciated motiles that were L. salmonis (Y) to the yearly mean number of motiles per fish across all species (X). Orange points are the speciated years (2002-2021) the curve was fit to; the purple point is the model-predicted 2001 value, applied to 2001's unidentified motiles. All chalimus and all unidentified copepodites are counted as L. salmonis in every year. Predicted 2001 proportion = 1.000 at X = 3.44.
