## Notes

Fig. S4 (Scenario 4). Beta regression (logit link) relating the yearly pooled proportion of speciated motiles that were L. salmonis to the yearly mean number of motiles per fish (all species). Unlike the Scenario 3 nls, the intercept is not forced through the origin and the fit carries an uncertainty band (grey, 95% simulated from the mean-model coefficient covariance). Orange points are the speciated fit years (2002-2021); the purple point is the predicted 2001 value and the blue point its lower 95% bound, which seeds the sensitivity run. 2001 proportion = 0.992 (point), 0.838 (lower).
