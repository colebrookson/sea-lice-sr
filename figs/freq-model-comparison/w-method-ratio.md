## Notes

Ratio of observed-cell to closed-form predicted motile lice per fish, by year and model structure. Values away from one are years in which the realized sampling design and the assumed random-effect Gaussian imply different means. The two methods differ both because of design weighting and because conditional modes are shrunk toward zero while the sigma^2/2 correction is not, so the ratio does not isolate either cause on its own.
