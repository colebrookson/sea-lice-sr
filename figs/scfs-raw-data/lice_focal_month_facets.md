## Notes

Focal-month highlight small-multiple, March through June. Each of the first four panels shows one focal month in colour against the full grey cloud of every other month; the fifth panel overlays all four focal months together. Points are jittered on year only so the y axis (lice per fish) stays exact; zeros are drawn small and behind as a faint baseline; non-focal months are mid-grey context. Focal colours from MoMAColors Klein. Broughton data through 2025.
